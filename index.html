03 / FEATURED SYSTEM
A CLOSER LOOK AT THE WASTE SORTER

PERCEPTION MEETS ACTUATION

From camera
to correct bin.

The automatic waste sorter is a prototype that joins a custom-trained YOLO model to a compact, servo-driven sorting mechanism.

The ESP32-CAM provides image capture while a laptop performs the model inference. The predicted waste class is translated into a movement decision for the collector and bin mechanism.

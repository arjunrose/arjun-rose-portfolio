A classic start / stop motor latch. Press START to energise the coil; the auxiliary contact holds it until STOP or RESET.

The motor output is de-energised. Press START to latch the run bit.
WHAT TO NOTICE

The auxiliary contact M0.0 is wired in parallel with START. Once the coil energises, that contact closes and keeps the output on after START is released.
